B. COCOMO ORGANIC MODE EFFORT FORMULA:
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
   System Mode: Organic (Small-to-medium team, familiar environment, flexible deadlines)
   Constants for Organic Mode:
   a_b = 2.4,  b_b = 1.05
   c_b = 2.5,  d_b = 0.38

   1. Basic Effort Formula (E_basic):
      E_basic = a_b * (KLOC)^b_b
      E_basic = 2.4 * (17.201)^1.05
      E_basic = 2.4 * 20.168 = 48.40 Person-Months (PM)

   2. Development Time Formula (D_dev):
      D_dev = c_b * (E_basic)^d_b

   3. Average Staff Size Formula (SS):
